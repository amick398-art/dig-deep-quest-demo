First expedition? Start here
  1. Open Supplies and claim two eligible free shovels, or buy a pack using simulated DIG.
  2. Choose a covered position in the Mine, then tap Excavate. Discovered metal needs the displayed DIG pickaxe.
  3. To test Boost, buy a simulated pass. In Tools, open Simulation fixture and deposit simulated funding.
  4. Use information tools before excavating. Resolve 35 positions, or purchase early Depth Pass, then confirm the next depth.
