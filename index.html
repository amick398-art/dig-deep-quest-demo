How to play

  1. Open Shop and claim two eligible free shovels, or buy a pack using simulated DIG.
  2. Choose a covered position in the Mine, then tap Excavate. Discovered metal needs the displayed DIG pickaxe.
  3. Only the DIG reward pool has Boost Mode. Buy its pass using DIG only; buying does not guarantee activation. Coverage applies when the DIG pool meets its approved funding conditions. Digging still uses shovels.
  4. Use information tools before excavating. Resolve 35 positions, or purchase early Depth Pass, then confirm the next depth.

Nothing connects to a live wallet. Your phone saves a separate local game; saves do not sync across devices.
